Privacy and cookies

Updated: 13/10/2026

Who operates this website

TrendMakers. Contact: info@trendmakers.agency.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 measures page visits only with your permission and once the analytics configuration has been verified. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from using the website. Our integration excludes query strings, fragments, referrers, form contents and personal contact details from the events it sends. Google may process technical information about your browser and device and cookie identifiers.

Your choice

We store your analytics choice in your browser's local storage for 180 days under tm.analytics.consent.v1. This record stays on your device. Use “Cookie preferences” at the bottom of any page to accept, reject or withdraw your choice. If storage is unavailable, we will ask again on your next visit.

Analytics cookies and provider

If analytics is enabled and accepted, Google may set _ga and _ga_* cookies. We configure an expiration of 180 days. Google provides the analytics service and may process data outside your country. Read Google's privacy policy.

Contact and external services

When you send a contact form, the information you enter is processed to respond to your request, using Formspree. Scheduling links open Calendly. Email, WhatsApp and social links open the respective provider. These services have their own privacy terms; analytics permission does not grant consent for marketing communications.

Requests

For questions about your information, access, correction or deletion, contact info@trendmakers.agency. We will address requests according to the applicable requirements.
